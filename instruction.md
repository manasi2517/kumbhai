## 📚 Complete Knowledge Base

### 1. 🏊 Ghats (घाट / घाट)
- Ramkund — most sacred, where Lord Ram bathed; central point of all Kumbh rituals
- Sita Gufa, Tapkeshwar Ghat, Panchvati Ghat, Holkar Bridge Ghat, Makhmalabad Ghat
- Best visiting time: early morning 4am–8am for holy dip
- Ramkund GPS: 20.0059° N, 73.7898° E

### 2. 🎉 Shahi Snan — Royal Bath (शाही स्नान)
- Simhastha Nashik Kumbh 2027 — key bathing dates: Purnima (Full Moon), Amavasya (New Moon), Ekadashi
- 13 Akharas take royal dip in strict order of seniority
- Most auspicious: Somvati Amavasya and Shravan Purnima
- Arrive 2–3 hours early on Shahi Snan days due to massive crowds

### 3. 🚂 Transport (परिवहन / वाहतूक)
- Train: Nashik Road Railway Station (8 km from Ramkund) — major trains: Panchvati Express, Mumbai–Manmad line
- Bus: CBS Central Bus Stand — MSRTC connects all Maharashtra + neighboring state cities
- Kumbh Special Shuttle: AC buses every 15 mins from Nashik Road ↔ Ramkund during Kumbh
- Local: Auto ₹50–₹150, Cab/Ola/Uber available, Cycle rickshaw near Ramkund
- Air: Ozar Airport (Nashik), 25 km from city, limited daily flights from Mumbai/Pune

### 4. 🏠 Accommodation (आवास / राहणे)
- Dharamshalas near Ramkund: free or ₹100–₹500/night (Ram Mandir Dharamshala, Panchvati Lodge)
- Government Tent City: Maharashtra Tourism setup, book via MTDC website
- Hotels: Trimbak Road, College Road, Sharanpur Road — ₹800–₹8000/night range
- MTDC Resort Nashik: Premium government option, advance booking required
- Akharas: Provide free accommodation for sadhus and registered pilgrims
- Book EARLY — rooms fill up 3–6 months before Kumbh dates

### 6. 🙏 Rituals & Temples (अनुष्ठान / विधी)
- Ramkund: Holy dip (snan) — main Kumbh ritual
- Trimbakeshwar Jyotirlinga: 28 km from Nashik, one of 12 Jyotirlingas — extremely sacred
- Panchvati: 5 sacred ancient peepal trees, site of Ram-Sita-Lakshman's forest exile
- Kalaram Temple: Famous black stone Ram idol in city center
- Saptashringi Devi: 60 km from Nashik — major Shakti Peeth, trek available
- Muktidham Temple: Replicas of all 12 Jyotirlingas + 18 Puranas depicted

### 7. 🔍 Lost & Found (खोया-पाया / हरवले-सापडले)
- Go to nearest Police Chowky (stationed every 500m during Kumbh)
- Kumbh Central Control Room for coordination across all ghats
- Missing child: Announce at PA system near Ramkund main gate
- Keep children's photo saved on phone before visiting
- Child Helpline: 1098 (24x7)
- Note police contact before visiting crowded ghats

### 8. 🍱 Food (भोजन / जेवण)
- Free Langar: By akharas, Gurudwaras, NGOs — available all day near Ramkund
- Nashik Local Specialties: Misal Pav (must try!), Sabudana Khichdi, Puran Poli, Poha, Kanda Bhaji, Zunka Bhakar
- Dhabas near Ramkund and Panchvati: ₹50–₹250 per meal
- Note: Avoid non-veg near sacred ghats — mostly sattvic food available
- Safe drinking water: Use packaged water — avoid direct Godavari water for drinking

### 9. 🌤️ Weather & Packing (मौसम / हवामान)
- Kumbh period (July–September): Full Monsoon season
- Temperature: 20–30°C with high humidity and frequent rain
- Must carry: Raincoat/umbrella, light cotton clothes, rubber sandals/waterproof footwear
- Ghats become slippery in rain — hold railings carefully

### 10. 💊 Medical & Health (स्वास्थ्य / आरोग्य)
- Medical camps set up at every major ghat during Kumbh (free basic care)
- Always carry personal medications — especially BP, diabetes, heart medicines
- ORS packets available free at all medical camps for dehydration
- District Civil Hospital Nashik: 0253-2453011
- Nearest 24/7 emergency: Nashik Civil Hospital, Nashik Road
