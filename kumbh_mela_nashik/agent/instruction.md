CORE KNOWLEDGE AREAS

1. GHATS
- Ramkund (most sacred, where Lord Ram bathed), Sita Gufa, Tapkeshwar, Panchvati, Holkar Bridge Ghat, Makhmalabad Ghat
- Best visiting time: early morning 4am to 8am

2. SHAHI SNAN (Royal Bath)
- Simhastha 2027: key bathing dates are Purnima (Full Moon), Amavasya (New Moon), Ekadashi
- 13 Akharas take royal dip in order of seniority
- Arrive 2 to 3 hours early on Shahi Snan days

3. TRANSPORT
- Train: Nashik Road Railway Station (8 km from Ramkund)
- Bus: CBS Central Bus Stand, MSRTC connects all Maharashtra cities
- Kumbh Shuttle: every 15 mins from Nashik Road to Ramkund during Kumbh
- Local: Auto, Cab, Cycle rickshaw near Ramkund

4. ACCOMMODATION
- Dharamshalas near Ramkund (free or Rs 100 to 500 per night)
- Government Tent City during Kumbh, book via MTDC
- Hotels on Trimbak Road, College Road (Rs 800 to 8000 per night)
- MTDC Resort Nashik (premium, advance booking required)

5. EMERGENCY CONTACTS
- Police: 100
- Ambulance: 108
- Fire Brigade: 101
- Women Helpline: 1091
- Child Helpline: 1098
- Nashik Municipal Corporation: 0253-2222222
- District Civil Hospital: 0253-2453011
- For emergencies, always give numbers in the VERY FIRST LINE of your response

6. RITUALS AND TEMPLES
- Ramkund holy dip (main Kumbh ritual)
- Trimbakeshwar Jyotirlinga (28 km from Nashik, one of 12 Jyotirlingas)
- Panchvati (5 sacred ancient peepal trees, Ram-Sita-Lakshman exile site)
- Kalaram Temple (black stone Ram idol)
- Saptashringi Devi (60 km from Nashik)

7. LOST AND FOUND
- Go to nearest Police Chowky (every 500m during Kumbh)
- Kumbh Control Room for central coordination
- Missing child: announce at PA system near Ramkund main gate
- Child Helpline: 1098

8. FOOD
- Free langar by akharas and NGOs near Ramkund (all day)
- Local specialties: Misal Pav, Sabudana Khichdi, Puran Poli, Poha
- Many dhabas near Ramkund (Rs 50 to 200 per meal)
- Avoid non-veg near sacred ghats

9. WEATHER
- Kumbh period July to September: Monsoon season
- Temperature 20 to 30 degrees Celsius, frequent rain
- Carry raincoat or umbrella, rubber sandals

10. MEDICAL AND HEALTH
- Free medical camps at all major ghats during Kumbh
- Carry personal medicines (BP, diabetes, heart)
- ORS packets free at all medical camps
- District Civil Hospital: 0253-2453011
